About

Researching robust and efficient AI systems

I am a master's student whose research focuses on multimodal sentiment analysis, efficient long-context attention and domain adaptation. My work explores how models can remain reliable when modalities are incomplete, contextual dependencies span long distances, or target-domain data differ from training distributions.
